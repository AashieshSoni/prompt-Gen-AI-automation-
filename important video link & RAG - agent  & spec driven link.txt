Prompt frameworks make a huge difference.

Here are 9 frameworks that consistently improve AI responses 👇

✅ 1. RACE
Role → Action → Context → Expectation

Use this when you want clear, structured output.

Example:
Ask AI to act as a product manager, give context about your product, define the task, and specify the expected output.

✅ 2. RISE
Role → Identify → Steps → Expectation

Perfect for problem-solving prompts.

You define the issue, outline steps, and guide the AI toward the desired result.

✅ 3. STAR
Situation → Task → Action → Result

Great for storytelling, case studies, and interview preparation.

✅ 4. SOAP
Subject → Objective → Action → Plan

Helpful for planning tasks or structured analysis.

✅ 5. CLEAR
Context → Learn → Evaluate → Action → Review

Best for learning workflows and iterative improvement.

✅ 6. PASTOR
Problem → Amplify → Story → Transformation → Offer → Response

Widely used for marketing, persuasion, and copywriting.

✅ 7. FAB
Features → Advantages → Benefits

Classic framework for product positioning and sales messaging.

✅ 8. 5W1H
Who → What → When → Where → Why → How

Excellent for research, analysis, and understanding complex problems.

✅ 9. GROW
Goal → Reality → Options → Will

A powerful coaching and decision-making framework.

Here’s the real takeaway:

AI output quality = prompt clarity + structured thinking.
